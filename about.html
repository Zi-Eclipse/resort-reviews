Our mission

Make resort work transparent — for everyone on the island

Resort jobs change lives, but the decision to take one is usually made blind: a contract, a few marketing photos, and a long flight. Staff deserve to know what the housing, food, management, and day-to-day reality are actually like before they commit.

We give workers a safe, anonymous place to share that truth — and give resorts a fair, verified way to respond and improve. Better information makes for better workplaces. That's the whole idea.
